Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Caddie is a golf trip planner: one shared link lets your crew vote on dates, courses and stays, split costs, and track scores. This policy describes what the app collects and what it never does — in plain language.

What we collect

  • Account information (only if you log in). Your email address, via Supabase Auth — either Google sign-in or an email/password you create. Logging in is optional: voting through a shared trip link never requires an account.
  • Trip content you and your crew enter. Trip names, destinations, golfer names and handicaps, date/course/stay options, votes, cost items, who's paid, and scores. Anyone with a trip's share link can view and edit this content — that's how the app is designed, so don't put sensitive personal information in a trip.
  • Course-search queries. When you search for courses near a destination, the destination text is sent to Google Places through our backend to return real course listings.
  • On-device data. Your login session, which golfer you vote as on each trip ("I am…"), and a pending login redirect are stored in your browser's local storage so the app remembers you.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We show no ads and do no cross-site or cross-app tracking.
  • We don't collect your location — course searches use the destination you type, not GPS.

Where data is stored

Account and trip data is stored with Supabase (Postgres database) in its US region. Course searches go through a Supabase-hosted function to the Google Places API.

Your rights

  • Trip owners can delete a trip (and all its votes, costs, and scores) from the app's Danger zone — this is immediate and irreversible.
  • Anyone can ask us to delete their account or trip data by emailing chadkoenig10@gmail.com. We'll confirm once it's done.
